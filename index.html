Filipino Classic

Chicken Adobo

Savory, tangy and a little sweet. The dish every Filipino home knows by heart.

  • Prep 10 min
  • Cook 40 min
  • Serves 4

Ingredients

  • 1 kg chicken, cut into pieces
  • 1/2 cup soy sauce
  • 1/3 cup cane vinegar
  • 1 head garlic, crushed
  • 3 bay leaves
  • 1 tsp whole peppercorns
  • 1 tbsp brown sugar
